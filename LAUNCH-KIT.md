# SHOMEI launch kit

## Product Hunt

**Name:** SHOMEI — Free & Private ID Photo Tools

**Tagline:** Create compliant ID photos without uploads, accounts, watermarks, or paid downloads.

**Description:**

SHOMEI is a free, privacy-first ID photo editor that runs entirely in your browser. Create passport, visa, résumé, and identity photos for popular document sizes worldwide. Remove or replace backgrounds, crop with face guides, take a photo directly from your camera, make print sheets, compress images, convert JPG/PNG/WebP, and calculate mm/px/DPI—all without uploading personal photos to a server.

**First comment:**

I built SHOMEI because many ID photo sites show a free preview and charge at download, while users must upload one of their most sensitive images. SHOMEI keeps processing on-device and makes every core feature free. I would love feedback on country-specific sizes, background removal quality, and the mobile workflow.

## Reddit

**Title:** I built a free passport/ID photo maker that processes everything locally

Many passport photo tools lock the final download behind a payment screen or require uploading a sensitive face photo. I built SHOMEI to do the opposite: no account, no upload, no watermark, and no paid download.

It supports common passport, visa and ID formats worldwide, background replacement, manual cleanup, camera capture, printable sheets, compression, format conversion, and DPI calculations. Processing happens in the browser.

I’m looking for honest feedback—especially on mobile usability, missing country formats, and difficult background-removal cases: https://sundata.tech/
