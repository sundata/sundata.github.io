35×45mm passport and visa photos

The 35×45mm format is common across passports, visas and identity documents, but head size and background rules differ by authority.

At 300 DPI

35×45mm converts to approximately 413×531 pixels. For online applications, always follow the specified pixel and file-size limits rather than DPI alone.
