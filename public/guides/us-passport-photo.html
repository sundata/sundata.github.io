US passport photo: 2×2 inches

A US passport photo is 2×2 inches (51×51mm). Use a recent, high-resolution photo with an evenly lit white or off-white background.

Quick checklist

  • Head height between 1 and 1⅜ inches (25–35mm)
  • Neutral expression, both eyes open
  • Face the camera directly
  • No filters, retouching or AI alteration of facial features
